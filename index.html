Silviu S. Craciunas

I am a Senior Principal Scientist at NXP Semiconductors and also hold a part-time Associate Professor position in the Department of Applied Mathematics and Computer Science at the Technical University of Denmark (DTU). Before joining NXP, I held research and architecture roles at TTTech Computertechnik AG and TTTech Auto AG in Vienna, Austria, where I worked on deterministic networking and real-time systems across industrial, aerospace, and automotive domains. My research focuses on predictable and safety-critical distributed systems, including Time-Sensitive Networking (TSN), real-time scheduling, and edge AI for embedded platforms. I am particularly interested in methods that combine real-time systems, deterministic networking, and edge computing to enable the next generation of dependable cyber-physical and autonomous systems.
